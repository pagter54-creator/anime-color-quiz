    Create a minimalist flat 2D vector color-palette abstraction image based on the three uploaded character images, arranged from left to right in order (1st image on the left, 2nd in the center, 3rd on the right).

Layout & Canvas:
- Wide 16:9 horizontal canvas with a uniform, solid light neutral gray background (#F2F2F2).
- Place three identical-sized tall vertical rectangles (each roughly 9:16 aspect ratio) side by side horizontally with wide, equal spacing between them and clean margins around the edges.
- Each rectangle must have sharp 90-degree corners, no borders, no outlines, and no drop shadows.

Color Block Rules (Inside Each Rectangle):
- Fill each vertical rectangle completely from top to bottom with flat, solid horizontal color stripes representing the corresponding character's silhouette and outfit colors from head to toe.
- Order the stripes anatomically from top to bottom: hair/headgear color at the top, skin tone for the face/neck, eyes/collar accents, upper outfit/top/jacket in the middle, waist/belt accents, lower outfit/skirt/pants/legs below, and socks/shoes at the very bottom.
- Adjust the vertical thickness of each horizontal stripe proportionally to how much visual height that part occupies on the character.
- Use 6 to 10 crisp horizontal bands per rectangle.

Strict Style Constraints:
- Purely flat solid colors only. Strictly NO gradients, NO shading, NO textures, NO blending, NO character details, and NO text or numbers anywhere.
